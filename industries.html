Industries We Serve

No two industries print the same way, which is why we don't sell a single product and call it universal.

What supplies to each industry

We provide solutions for all industry verticals — automobile, ancillary, manufacturing, logistics, retail, electronics, garment, healthcare, FMCG, pharmaceuticals, steel, utility and banking applications. Below is what we typically supply to each.

01

Automobile

Durable TTR-printed part and asset labels for inventory and shop-floor tracking.

02

Ancillary

Barcode labels and ribbons for component traceability across supplier networks.

03

Manufacturing

Heat- and chemical-resistant TTR labels for production lines and work-in-progress tracking.

04

Logistics

Shipping and barcode labels, plus wide-compatibility ribbons for high-volume dispatch.

05

Retail

POS billing systems, thermal paper rolls, and colour labels for pricing and promotions.

06

Electronics

TTO ribbons for coding on packaging and components, with clean edge definition.

07

Garment

Wash care ribbons for cotton and blended fabric labels, size tags and brand tags.

08

Healthcare

Water-, heat- and chemical-resistant labels for sample, equipment and inventory tracking.

09

FMCG

TTO overprint ribbons for batch and expiry coding on flexible packaging and cartons.

10

Pharmaceuticals

TTO and TTR ribbons for compliant, smudge-free batch and expiry coding on pouches and cartons.

11

Steel

Heavy-duty, heat-resistant TTR ribbons built for harsh industrial environments.

12

Utility

Durable asset and meter labels built to survive outdoor exposure.

13

Banking

Thermal POS rolls and colour POS rolls for receipts and transaction records.
