About Us

Not a product catalogue with a sales number attached. We walk in with a fix.

Who we are

Primoo isn't a product catalogue with a sales number attached. We're built to solve a specific kind of problem: a business whose labelling, coding or billing setup isn't working as well as it should. That might mean mismatched ribbons burning through inventory faster than necessary, or a POS system that can't keep up with a lunch rush. Either way, we don't walk in with a single SKU — we walk in with a fix.

Our core value proposition is simple yet powerful: we do not merely sell products; we deliver complete solutions that help businesses improve efficiency, productivity and operational performance.

Our value proposition

We're measured on outcomes, not on units shipped. Pricing reflects the value a solution delivers, not just its material cost, and because our delivery process is standardized across every client, the reliability is the same whether an order is ten rolls or ten thousand.

By building a strong partner ecosystem and standardizing our delivery processes, we ensure consistency, reliability and customer satisfaction at every stage.

Our products and solutions, at a glance

  • POS Thermal Paper Roll — premium thermal paper rolls, coloured POS rolls and complete POS solutions for smooth, jam-free billing.
  • Labels — paper, synthetic and vibrant colour labels for branding, coding, identification and organization.
  • Thermal Transfer Ribbons (TTR) — high-performance ribbons for sharp, durable and long-lasting prints.
  • Wash Care Ribbons — high-quality ribbons for wash care labels with durable, long-lasting prints.
  • Thermal Transfer Overprint (TTO) — premium thermal transfer overprint for superior coding and marking.
  • Compatible & Reliable — every solution works with all major printer brands manufactured globally.
